# Datasets: Goodness-of-Fit Testing for the Pareto Type-I Distribution Based on a Mean Residual Life Characterization

This repository contains the two real-world datasets used in the paper **“Goodness-of-Fit Testing for the Pareto Type-I Distribution Based on a Mean Residual Life Characterization”**, available on *arXiv* (2026).

## Datasets

### Dataset 1: **Danish Fire Insurance Dataset:** Fire insurance claim amounts from Denmark (1980–1990).

### Dataset 2: **Delhi Air Pollution Dataset:** Daily PM2.5 observations from Delhi, India (November–December, 2020–2024).

## Reference

Nila, S., Das, I., & Balakrishna, N. (2026). Goodness-of-fit testing for the Pareto type-I distribution based on a mean residual life characterization. *arXiv preprint*, arXiv:2609.04933.

**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04933
